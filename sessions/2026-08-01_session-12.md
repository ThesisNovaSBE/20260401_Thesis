### Fairness gaps
- Recall gap: 0.021 (worst: 18-40) — acceptable
- Precision gap: 0.070 (worst: 70+)
- AUROC gap: 0.094 (best: 18-40, worst: 70+) — **key finding**

### Interpretation

The AUROC of 0.676 for Stage 2 (text only) is slightly below Stage 1's structured AUROC of 0.706. This is expected and actually strengthens the thesis argument: neither modality dominates, making the cross-modal discordance analysis in Stage 3 genuinely motivated.

The 70+ AUROC gap (0.094) is the most important fairness finding. Clinical Longformer reads younger patients' discharge notes better. Older patients' notes likely contain more fragmented, atypical language (frailty, delirium, polypharmacy) that is harder for the model to generalise from. This directly motivates the `frailty_markers` and `cognition` categories in Stage 3.

High recall (90.8%) confirms the high-recall mandate: virtually all true readmissions are flagged by Stage 1 and survive Stage 2 confirmation.

ECE of 0.153 is imperfect calibration. One sentence caveat warranted in the methodology section.
